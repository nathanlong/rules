---
title: Skull King Scorecard
description: A digital scorecard for Skull King that tracks bids, tricks won, and bonus points.
date: 2024-09-18
---

1. Add each player's name in seating order, then choose **Start**.
2. After each round, choose **Record round**. For each player, enter their **bid**, the **tricks won**, and any **bonus** points. Recording moves on to the next player automatically.
3. Select any cell in the scorecard to correct an earlier round.
4. Scores are saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back to a game in progress.

**Scoring:** each round shows the bid and tricks won, the round's score, and the running total. The current leader is marked with a crown.
